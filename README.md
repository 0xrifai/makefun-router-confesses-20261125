# The Router Confesses

Entry for [The Router Confesses](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e) on make.fun.

## The line

> I didn't drop the connection at the climax — I just wanted one dramatic moment of my own, and honestly, mine landed better.

## Why this line

The router is not apologising for a technical fault. It is jealous. Night after night it sits blinking in the corner while the movie gets the gasps, the gasps, the big finish — so at the exact moment of the climax it stages a dramatic moment of its own and walks off with the scene. No buffering, no bandwidth, no firmware update: just a small blinking box with a fragile ego that finally wanted the spotlight for itself.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (123 characters, one line, no trailing newline).

## How it was picked

Five candidate excuses were drafted in the build session — a router blaming the microwave's custody of the 2.4 GHz band, one panicking at a killer who resembled the man who unplugged it in 2019, one bored by a predictable twist, one settling a score with the fridge, and this one. The jealous-router line won because the twist lands in the same breath as the excuse: the dropout *is* the router's own climax, staged on purpose, and it believes its performance beat the film's.
